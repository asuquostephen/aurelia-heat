Aurelia Heat

Aurelia Heat is a top-down open-world crime game set in Port Aurelia, a coastal city of avenues, a central park and a harbour. You arrive with empty pockets and one contact: Marlo Venn, a fixer who pays cash for jobs. Complete six chained story jobs, run taxi fares in free roam, and stay ahead of the patrols.

Moving and driving

Move on foot with W, A, S, D or the arrow keys, and hold Shift to sprint. Press E next to any car to get in — or to steal it, if someone is driving — and E again to get out. Cars drive with the same movement keys; different classes have different top speeds and take hull damage in collisions.

Jobs and earning

Press M to start Marlo's next job: reaching landmarks, delivering a stolen coupe, beating a delivery clock, escaping a frame-up, clearing rival crew gunmen, and a final three-bag collection downtown. Jobs pay cash on completion. Between jobs, pick up pedestrians waving for a taxi and drive them to their destination for a fare. Press L to toggle Dusk Mode: jobs and fares pay a 25 percent bonus at dusk, and wanted heat fades faster — but the city is darker.

Wanted level and roadblocks

Crimes raise your heat, shown as one to five wanted stars: carjacking, theft, assault, gunfire, hit-and-runs and ramming patrol cars all count. Patrol cars chase, ram, and corner you; if they pin you down, the bust meter fills and you wake up at the clinic, fined. At three stars or more, patrols also set up roadblocks — three police cars parked across the avenue ahead of you. Ram through, swerve off-road, or turn back, but do not stop in front of one. Heat only decays while no patrol can see you, so break line of sight and stay hidden.

Combat and other keys

Aim with the mouse and click to fire your sidearm; press Space to punch. Watch your health and ammunition on the heads-up display, and collect health, ammo and cash pickups around the city. Press K to save your progress, and P or Escape to pause — the game also pauses itself if you switch to another tab.
